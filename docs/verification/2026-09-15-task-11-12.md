The health-load figure is a failure-routing baseline, not a production chat-throughput
claim. Chat capacity and monthly SLO compliance must be measured in the selected production
environment because they include the configured LM Studio capacity and network path.
Running two OCR workers on the 8 GiB single-node Kind cluster exposed the capacity bottleneck:
an image-heavy scheduled scan exceeded the prior 2 GiB per-container limit and was OOM-killed.
The local real environment therefore uses one worker with a 4 GiB limit; the production
example keeps two workers but declares 1 GiB requests/4 GiB limits and requires appropriately
sized nodes. API and Web remain at two replicas for availability.

The capacity-adjusted final local release is Helm revision 20: two Ready backend Pods,
two Ready Web Pods and one Ready 4 GiB worker. All 168 scan events and four embedding
events were processed with zero pending/dead-letter events. The final real-course smoke
passed and emitted `course_tutor_chat_time_to_first_event_seconds` from the serving Agent.
