## Chapter retrieval and sparse-PDF OCR repair

- The initial PDF version indexed only 16 chunks and had no chunks on Unit 1's
  physical PDF pages 7–9, even though its outline exposed that chapter. The parser
  had applied OCR only when the *entire PDF* lacked text.
- Pipeline 1.5.0 OCRs sparse pages individually and indexes numeric physical page
  anchors. A single-file re-ingestion produced 84 chunks (69 OCR-recovered pages)
  in immutable version e501a379-a074-479f-a308-27bffa4bba7b, then published it.
  The broad NAS source root was explicitly narrowed to the one previously bound PDF
  for this replay; no other textbook was ingested into the course.
- Practice now sends a stable outline-node ID to Agent. Agent validates the published
  book/source binding and filters Qdrant by the node's exact page interval, not by the
  ambiguous “Unit 1” label. The new Unit 1 five-question request reached READY
  after one safe validator retry; its three cited chunks are all from pages 7–9 of
  the selected PDF. The learner-facing set contains five questions.
- The generated output remains model-dependent: an earlier five-question attempt
  failed strict answer validation, and a later attempt passed after a retry. Do not
  count this one successful run as the human-approved golden-set quality gate.
